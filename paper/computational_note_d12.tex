\begin{abstract}
We record a verified computational scaffold for the discriminant-12 S-shaped Prym eigenform prototype of McMullen.
The package supplies an exact real-multiplication endomorphism and eigenplane projector with residual~0;
plane-preserving corrected Rauzy generators;
an independent geometric construction of $S(1,-2)$;
and dual Rauzy path evaluation whose positive Lyapunov sum lies in an interval containing $8/5$ under a controlled QR error model.
Individual non-tautological exponents remain experimental.
The sum $8/5$ on $H(4)^{\mathrm{odd}}$ is due to Chen--M\"oller; this note does not claim that theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Prym eigenform loci of McMullen supply infinite families of primitive rank-one affine invariant submanifolds.
For $D=12$, $\Omega E_{12}(4)$ is a single closed $\mathrm{GL}^+(2,\mathbb{R})$-orbit in $H^{\mathrm{odd}}(4)$.
Chen--M\"oller give the sum of positive Lyapunov exponents as $8/5$ on this locus.
This note documents a machine-checkable computational pipeline. It does not re-prove Chen--M\"oller.

\section{Real multiplication}

Let
\[
T=\begin{pmatrix}2&0&2&0\\0&2&0&2\\1&0&0&0\\0&1&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Verification yields residual~0 for $T^2-2T-2I=0$.
With $\lambda=1+\sqrt{3}$, the projector $P_\lambda=(T-\bar\lambda I)/(\lambda-\bar\lambda)$ is idempotent of rank~2.
The prototype period $(\lambda,0,1,0)$ is an exact eigenvector.
Corrected Rauzy generators preserve the eigenplane to residual $\sim10^{-16}$.

\section{Geometric construction}

An independent pure-Python construction realises $S(1,-2)$ as an octagon from two unit rectangles and a $\lambda\times\lambda$ square.
The surface lies in $H(4)$, genus~3, with period residual $\sim10^{-16}$ against $T$ without forced re-projection.

\section{Dual Rauzy evaluation}

On the zippered-rectangle presentation, dual transition matrices are exact integers.
Under ball arithmetic with controlled QR inflation, the positive-part sum lies in an interval containing $8/5$.
This is a path-local consistency check under a stated error model, not a proof of Chen--M\"oller.

\section{What is not claimed}

Formal bounds on individual non-tautological exponents; authorship of the sum $8/5$; exhaustive geometric Rauzy class enumeration beyond the presentations used here.
