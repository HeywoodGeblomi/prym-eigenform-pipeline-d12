\title{Discriminant independence of the non-tautological\\
Kontsevich--Zorich spectrum on residual-0 Model~A$\pm$\\
Prym Teichm\"uller curves in $H_3(4)^{\mathrm{odd}}$}

\author{Heywood Geblomi}

\date{}

\begin{abstract}
We prove that the area Siegel--Veech constant (and therefore the non-tautological
Lyapunov sum) is independent of the discriminant on every Teichm\"uller curve in
the odd component of the stratum $H(4)$, including all residual-0 Model~A$\pm$
Prym Teichm\"uller curves. The result is a direct corollary of the Chen--M\"oller
non-varying theorem for genus-3 strata together with the Eskin--Kontsevich--Zorich
formula: the sum of Lyapunov exponents on $H(4)^{\mathrm{odd}}$ equals $8/5$,
hence $c_{\mathrm{area}}=18/(5\pi^2)$ and $\lambda_2+\lambda_3=3/5$ for every
such curve, independently of $D$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Prym eigenforms and the Teichm\"uller curves they generate were introduced by
McMullen~\cite{McM06}. Their classification inside $H(4)$ is due to
Lanneau--Nguyen~\cite{LN14}. Complete periodicity is proved in~\cite{LN16}.

Chen and M\"oller~\cite{CM12} established that the sum of Lyapunov exponents is
non-varying on every stratum in genus~3 except the principal stratum. For the
odd component of $H(4)$ the constant value is $8/5$. Combined with the
Eskin--Kontsevich--Zorich formula~\cite{EKZ14}, this forces a universal area
Siegel--Veech constant on the whole component.

\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:main}
Let $\mathcal{C}$ be any Teichm\"uller curve in $H_3(4)^{\mathrm{odd}}$
(in particular any residual-0 Model~A$\pm$ Prym Teichm\"uller curve of any
admissible discriminant $D$). Then
\[
c_{\mathrm{area}}(\mathcal{C})=\frac{18}{5\pi^2}
\]
and
\[
\lambda_2+\lambda_3=\frac{3}{5}.
\]
Both quantities are independent of $D$.
\end{theorem}

\section{Proof}

Chen--M\"oller~\cite[Theorem~1.1]{CM12} prove that the sum of Lyapunov exponents
is non-varying on every stratum in genus~3 except the principal stratum.
For $\Omega\mathcal{M}_3(4)^{\mathrm{odd}}$ the stratum sum equals $8/5$
(via the Eskin--Masur--Zorich Siegel--Veech algorithm and EKZ; see also the
slope argument in~\cite[\S6]{CM12} showing $s(C)=9$ for every Teichm\"uller
curve in the component). Consequently every Teichm\"uller curve
$\mathcal{C}\subset H_3(4)^{\mathrm{odd}}$ satisfies
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{3}\lambda_i(\mathcal{C})=\frac{8}{5}.
\]

The Eskin--Kontsevich--Zorich formula on $H(4)$ reads
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{3}\lambda_i
=
\frac{2}{5}+\frac{\pi^2}{3}\,c_{\mathrm{area}}.
\]
Solving for the area Siegel--Veech constant yields
\[
c_{\mathrm{area}}(\mathcal{C})=\frac{18}{5\pi^2}.
\]
Subtracting the tautological exponent $\lambda_1=1$ gives
\[
\lambda_2+\lambda_3=\frac{3}{5}.
\]
Neither quantity depends on the discriminant of a residual-0 real-multiplication
structure on $\mathcal{C}$.

\begin{remark}
The same conclusion holds for every Teichm\"uller curve in the component, residual-0
or not. Individual non-tautological exponents $\lambda_2$ and $\lambda_3$ may still
vary; only their sum is locked by the stratum.
\end{remark}
