# Path 2 — THEOREM PROVED

## Theorem

Let $\mathcal{C}_D$ be any Teichmüller curve in $H_3(4)^{\mathrm{odd}}$ (in particular any residual-0 Model A± Prym Teichmüller curve). Then

$$
c_{\mathrm{area}}(\mathcal{C}_D) = \frac{18}{5\pi^2}
$$
independently of $D$, and

$$
\lambda_2 + \lambda_3 = \frac{3}{5}.
$$

## Proof

Chen–Möller (Geom. Topol. 16 (2012), arXiv:1104.3932, Theorem 1.1):
the sum of Lyapunov exponents is non-varying on every stratum in genus 3 except the principal stratum. For $H(4)^{\mathrm{odd}}$ the constant value is $L=8/5$.

Every residual-0 Model A± curve $\mathcal{C}_D$ lies in this component, so

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{3}\lambda_i(\mathcal{C}_D) = \frac{8}{5}.
$$

EKZ on $H(4)$:

$$
\sum\lambda_i = \frac{2}{5} + \frac{\pi^2}{3}\,c_{\mathrm{area}}.
$$

Solving yields $c_{\mathrm{area}}=18/(5\pi^2)$ and $\lambda_2+\lambda_3=3/5$, independent of $D$.
